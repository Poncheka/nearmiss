Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 September 2026

Near Miss shows you the times you and your friends were in the same place, sometimes years before you met. To do that it needs to know where you have been. This page explains exactly what leaves your phone, what does not, and how to get rid of it.

The short version. Your photos and videos never leave your phone. Near Miss reads the date, time and coordinates recorded inside them and sends only those. Anything from the last two days is ignored. Places you mark as hidden are deleted before they are stored. Only people you have added as friends can see a near miss involving you. Nothing is sold, and there are no ads or third-party trackers.

Who we are

Near Miss is an independent app made by Jeff Ponchick. For anything on this page, write to hello@nearmiss.io and a person will answer.

What we collect, and why

WhatWhy
Email address To sign you in, and so friends who already have your address in their contacts can find you. If you sign in with Apple and use Hide My Email, we only ever see the relay address.
Name, username, profile photo So friends recognise you. All optional except the username.
Photo and video metadata The date, time and GPS coordinates stored inside each photo or video, plus its identifier on your device so the same one isn't counted twice. This is what near misses are found from.
Hidden places The centre point and radius of any place you mark hidden, so we can throw away everything recorded inside it.
Contact fingerprints A one-way SHA-256 hash of each email address in your contacts, to tell you which of them already use Near Miss.

Device location Read once, only in the moment you tap "I'm here now" while marking a hidden place. Near Miss does not track your location in the background and asks for no such permission.

What we do not collect

  • Your photos and videos. The files never leave your phone. We read their metadata and send nothing else.
  • Your contact list. Names, phone numbers and addresses stay on your phone. Only irreversible hashes of email addresses are sent, and the server answers only with people who have registered that same address.
  • Your movements in real time. There is no background location, no beacons, no Wi-Fi scanning.
  • Advertising identifiers. There is no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no tracking across other apps or websites.

The rules that protect you

  • Nothing from the last two days. Photo metadata newer than two days is never uploaded, and a near miss stays invisible until two days after the day it happened. Near Miss can tell you where you were, never where you are.
  • Hidden places are dropped on arrival. When you mark a place hidden, anything already stored inside it is deleted and anything later recorded inside it is rejected before it is written.
  • Friends only. A near miss is visible to the two people in it. Nobody can browse your history, and there are no public profiles.
  • No exact addresses. A near miss shows a point on a map and a place name looked up on your own phone. It does not tell your friend where you live or work.

Who else touches your data

We use a small number of services to run the app. They process data on our instructions and are not allowed to use it for their own purposes.

  • Supabase: the database and sign-in system, hosted on Amazon Web Services. This is where everything in the table above is stored.
  • Apple and Google: only if you choose to sign in with them, and only to confirm who you are.
  • Apple Maps: to turn coordinates into a place name. This lookup happens on your phone; only the resulting name is saved.

We do not sell your data, share it with data brokers, or hand it to advertisers. We would disclose data if legally compelled to, and will tell you if we are allowed to.

How long we keep it

Photo metadata and near misses are kept while your account exists, because that is the product. Everything else is deleted with your account.

Deleting things

  • One near miss: mark it "we were together" or "not interesting" and it disappears from your feed.
  • A place: add it as a hidden place and everything recorded there is deleted.
  • All your location data: in the app, under your profile, "Delete my photo data". This removes every stored point and every near miss built from it, immediately.
  • Your whole account: in the app, or email hello@nearmiss.io. Everything is removed within 30 days, and backups age out within 90.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, and we will do it. If you are in the UK, EU or EEA, our legal basis is your consent for reading photo metadata and contacts (which you can withdraw at any time in your phone's settings), and legitimate interest for keeping the service running and secure. If you are in California, we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising any right.

Children

Near Miss is not for people under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them. If you believe a child has an account, write to us and we will remove it.

Security

Everything travels over HTTPS and is stored encrypted at rest. Access to other people's rows is blocked at the database level, not just in the app, so a bug in the app cannot expose someone else's history. We are a small project, though, and no system is perfect. If you find a problem, please tell us at hello@nearmiss.io before telling anyone else.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
